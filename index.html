About this course

This course is designed for dental nurses developing their knowledge of implant dentistry. It covers anatomy and physiology, patient assessment and treatment planning, the implant treatment journey, instruments and materials, peri-implant care, infection control, legal and professional responsibilities, medical emergencies, and advanced surgical procedures.

Each module includes structured learning content, a glossary of key terms, and a module quiz drawn from a bank of questions. Modules unlock sequentially as you progress, and a final assessment covering the full course is available once all ten modules have been completed.
